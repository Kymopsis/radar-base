# ESPHome

> Planned after the Home Assistant MQTT integration.

An ESPHome external component will let you describe Radar Base in YAML like any other ESPHome device. It will use
the same results as the app and MQTT (people, areas, fall, breathing and heart rate), so you can mix them with your
own ESPHome sensors and automations.

Until then, use [Home Assistant over MQTT](home-assistant), or read the JSON results directly ([JSON and API](json-api)).

Volume, pre-flashed modules or custom firmware: [Kymopsis B2B](https://kymopsis.com/b2b/?utm_source=radar-site&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=radar-base)
