# Hotspot fallback

Radar Base opens its own Wi-Fi network only when it has to: when no home network is saved yet, or when the saved one
has not been reachable for 30 seconds. It is meant for first setup and for demos away from home.

## Connect

1. On your phone or laptop, join the network **KymRadarBase-xxxx** (password `kymradar`).
2. The setup page usually opens by itself. If not, open **http://192.168.4.1**.

While connected, your phone has no internet through this network; that is normal. Most phones show a notice and
keep mobile data available.

## Set up home Wi-Fi from here

Device settings → Home Wi-Fi → choose your network → password → Connect. Then reconnect your phone to your home
Wi-Fi and open http://kymradar.local ([Home Wi-Fi](wifi)).

## When it switches off

As soon as the home network works and no phone is connected to the hotspot.
